## 📈 Actual Payment History & Allocation
The mandatory Repayment Strategy dictates that payments must be allocated strictly in this order across all arrears installments:

   1. Fees → 2. Penalties → 3. Interest → 4. Principal

## Payment 1: R11,600.00
This lump sum clears the first three months of arrears completely and leaves an overpayment for September:

* Months Covered: June (R3,400), July (R3,400), and August (R3,400) are fully paid.
* Total for Arrears: R10,200.00 processed.
* Excess Remaining: R1,400.00. This excess goes towards:
	1) paying off the interest owed from the 1st September till the current date. To estimate this, you divide the interest due by the days in period to get the interest due per day. And multiply that by the number of days to the current days.
	2) reducing the principal due on the loan. Apply Remaining Excess and immediately reduce the principal due of the loan.
	3) Dynamic Re-calculation: Recalculate interest on the fly based on the exact number of days in the cycle using the newly reduced principal balance

Please remember that step two downwards applies to future records. You don't charge fee, penalties and interest if the date is in future.
